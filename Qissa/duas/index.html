Dua of the day

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

(Rabbi’ghfir warham wa anta khayrur-rāḥimīn)

O My Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the Best of those who show mercy.

Qur’an – Surah Al-Mu’minun (23:118)

Dua at the Time of Iftar

A beautiful dua to recite when breaking your fast.

Dua for Sehri (Fasting)

Intention dua to recite when beginning the fast.

Daily Adhkars

Dua for Forgiveness & MercySeeking Allah's boundless mercy and pardon.
Dua for Guidance & PietyAsking for guidance, righteousness, and contentment.
Dua for ParentsPraying for mercy upon our parents as they raised us.
Dua Before EatingRecited before starting any meal or drink.
Dua After Completing a MealThanking Allah for providing food and sustenance.

During Morning

Morning Remembrance (Asbahna)Acknowledging the new morning under Allah's sovereignty.
Dua for Beneficial KnowledgeAsking for useful knowledge, pure provision, and accepted deeds.
Sayyid al-Istighfar (Master Supplication)The supreme morning prayer seeking forgiveness.
Dua for Complete ProtectionSeeking refuge from harm in the earth and heavens.
Dua for Well-Being (Afiyah)Asking for health and safety in religion, worldly life, and family.

During Evening

Evening Remembrance (Amsayna)Entering the evening with praise and gratitude to Allah.
Dua for Relief from AnxietySeeking protection from worry, grief, and hardship.
Dua for Sunset & Night PeaceAsking for the goodness of the coming night.
Dua for GratitudeThanking Allah for all blessings bestowed throughout the day.
Dua for Laylatul QadrAsking Allah for pardon during the blessed nights.

During Night

Dua Before SleepingRecited when lying down to rest for the night.
Ayat al-Kursi ProtectionSeeking divine guardianship throughout the night.
Dua Upon Waking UpPraising Allah who gives life after sleep.
